Join the Movement for Cleaner, Greener Streets

CleanStreet connects community members, volunteers, and local authorities to transform public spaces into clean, safe, and beautiful areas for everyone to enjoy.

Our Mission

CleanStreet is a community-driven initiative that brings together residents, volunteers, and local administrators to maintain clean and green neighborhoods. We believe that clean environments foster healthier communities and improve quality of life for all residents. Whether you want to report an issue, volunteer for cleanup activities, or manage community initiatives, CleanStreet provides the platform to make a tangible difference in your local area.

Choose Your Role

Select your role to get started with CleanStreet. Each role plays a vital part in keeping our communities clean and beautiful.

Community Member

Volunteer
